THE ROAD TO THE GRAND BEE

They didn’t take our words.

  1. The Lower Comb. A free makeover, a hopeful bee, and ten flowers to begin again. “Getting help to learn is brave. Pretending someone else’s answer is yours is different.”
  2. The dishonest champion. Early in the story, we see the FireBee hiding something and claiming a victory he did not earn. S’Pling resolves to grow honestly. The method stays a mystery.
  3. A suspicious signal. Between contests, the FireBee watches S’Pling rise. Later scenes reveal an accomplice looking up words and whispering answers through a transmitter.
  4. The stolen final. S’Pling’s correct spellings still count. A story scene reveals how the Baron cheats to take the title.
  5. The honest rematch. Earn the parts for a fictional signal blocker, expose the transmitter, then beat the Baron by spelling. The blocker removes his help; it never supplies answers.

These are story beats for future cutscenes, not playable levels yet.
